Join Future Scientific Leaders

Express interest in research training, mentorship, student chapters or interdisciplinary collaboration.

Who may be interested?

  • Undergraduate students exploring research.
  • Master's and MPhil students developing research independence.
  • Doctoral researchers pursuing advanced research and leadership.
  • Researchers and mentors interested in interdisciplinary collaboration.
  • Student groups interested in establishing an international chapter.

Before applying

Prepare a short profile describing your academic stage, research interests, relevant skills, preferred research pillar and what you hope to contribute. Participation should be subject to programme capacity, mentor availability, project requirements and the network's approved procedures.
