Researcher pathways

The pathways describe programme levels and expected development focus. They are not academic degrees, employment grades or guarantees of appointment.

Programme activities

  • Research methods and reproducibility training
  • Scientific programming and computational workflows
  • Mentor-guided research projects
  • Interdisciplinary seminars and journal clubs
  • Research communication and publication development
  • International collaboration and student chapters
