International Student Chapters

A proposed framework for student-led local communities connected to the wider research network.

Purpose

International Student Chapters can provide a local point of connection for research discussions, peer learning, seminars, collaborative activities and communication with the network's laboratories and mentors.

Suggested chapter activities

  • Monthly research reading groups and journal clubs.
  • Scientific writing, presentation and reproducibility workshops.
  • Student research showcases and interdisciplinary seminars.
  • Connections to mentor-led projects and laboratory opportunities.
  • Collaborative events with other chapters.

Chapter establishment

Each chapter should have an approved faculty or research mentor, a student coordination team, a documented activity plan, transparent membership criteria and an annual activity report. Do not announce a chapter as active until its leadership and approval are confirmed.
